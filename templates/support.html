❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a project?

Log in, then click New Project in the navigation bar. Fill in the details and submit.

How do I update my project progress?

Go to your project detail page and add milestones to track your progress. You can also update the project stage.

How does the Celebration Wall work?

When you set your project stage to Completed, it automatically appears on the Celebration Wall.

How do I collaborate with others?

Visit any project and click Raise Hand to Collaborate. The project owner can then accept or decline your request.

Can I delete my project?

Yes. Go to your project, click Edit, and scroll down to find the delete option.
